# Code of Conduct / コミュニティ行動規範

We welcome constructive participation from researchers, students, educators, developers and contributors across backgrounds and experience levels.

- Be respectful and inclusive; no harassment, discrimination, bullying or personal attacks.
- Critique scientific assumptions, code and evidence—not individuals.
- Identify uncertainty; do not misrepresent synthetic results, source rights, experimental provenance or authorship.
- Follow licensing and privacy obligations.
- Keep issues and reviews focused, reproducible and collaborative.

Maintainers may edit, hide or close disruptive contributions and may restrict repeated abusive participation. Report conduct concerns through GitHub's available non-public maintainer contact channels when possible; avoid posting private details publicly.

異なる専門分野・経験年数を尊重し、検証可能な根拠にもとづく建設的な議論を行ってください。
